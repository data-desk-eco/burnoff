About this map

This map detects gas flaring at LNG facilities using Sentinel-2 shortwave infrared (SWIR) imagery. Thermal anomalies are identified where B12 (2190nm) and B11 (1610nm) reflectance exceeds background levels, indicating active combustion. Click any detection to view the underlying SWIR imagery.

INSPIRATION

Hu et al. (2023) An approach to detect gas flaring sites using Sentinel-2 MSI and NOAA-20 VIIRS images. Int. J. Appl. Earth Obs. Geoinf.

Wu et al. (2022) Monitoring gas flaring in Texas using time-series Sentinel-2 MSI and Landsat-8 OLI images. Int. J. Appl. Earth Obs. Geoinf.
