🔥 BURNOFF

Detects gas flares and other heat sources using Sentinel-2 shortwave infrared imagery. Flares appear bright in SWIR bands due to thermal emission.

Click Detect to download and process Sentinel-2 imagery for the current view. Click any detection to view the satellite imagery and flare analysis.

Detection results sync peer-to-peer between all open Burnoff sessions and are saved locally. When you run a detection, peers automatically split the work and share results in real time.

Inspiration

Faruolo et al. (2024) The DAFI v2 algorithm for gas flare detection
